Refund Policy

Last updated: February 13, 2025 | Effective immediately

At JeetoPlay, we strive to provide a fair and transparent gaming experience. This Refund Policy outlines the circumstances under which refunds may be issued.

1. Eligible Refunds

✅ Refunds Are Applicable For:

  • Technical Failures: If a match is disrupted due to server-side technical issues on JeetoPlay's end, affecting gameplay or result submission
  • Unauthorized Transactions: If a deposit was made without your authorization (requires verification)
  • Duplicate Charges: If the same deposit was charged multiple times due to a payment gateway error
  • Match Cancellation: If a match is cancelled by mutual consent before gameplay starts, entry fees are refunded to Deposit Balance
  • Opponent No-Show: If your matched opponent fails to share room credentials or join the game, your entry fee is refunded
  • Admin-Cancelled Matches: If an admin cancels a disputed match where evidence is inconclusive, both players receive a full refund

2. Non-Refundable Scenarios

❌ Refunds Are NOT Applicable For:

  • Lost Matches: Entry fees for matches you've lost are not refundable, as the outcome was determined by skill
  • Used Deposits: Money already used to join tournaments or Ludo battles
  • Voluntary Forfeit: If you abandon or disconnect from an active match by choice
  • Rule Violations: If your account is terminated due to fraud, multi-accounting, or violating our Terms of Service
  • Referral Bonuses: Bonuses earned through the referral program are non-refundable
  • Withdrawal Reversals: Completed withdrawals cannot be reversed or refunded

3. Refund Process

To request a refund:

  1. Contact Support: Email us at support@jeetoplay.in with your registered email, match details, and reason for refund
  2. Provide Evidence: Include screenshots or transaction IDs as applicable
  3. Review: Our team will review your request and respond within 48 hours
  4. Resolution: If approved, refunds are credited to your JeetoPlay Deposit Balance

5. Disputes Involving Refunds

In cases where a match result is disputed:

  • If the admin resolves the dispute in your favour, you receive the full winnings
  • If the admin finds the dispute inconclusive, both players may receive a refund of their entry fees
  • If you are found to have submitted false evidence, no refund will be issued and your account may be penalized
